# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are parents choosing practical offline classes for children, adult learners choosing a course for themselves, and mixed families comparing directions, locations, and trial options. They arrive with a practical goal: understand what "Практика" teaches, choose a direction, find a convenient branch, and leave a request for a trial lesson or consultation.

## Product Purpose

"Практика" is a unified digital storefront for an offline education center with several directions: foreign languages, driving school, robotics, school subjects, creativity, and digital skills. The site must communicate that learning happens through action, attempts, feedback, and continued practice, not through a dry academic system.

Success means the homepage clearly explains the brand, direction cards lead to their own landing pages, the audience switcher helps people choose between adult and child scenarios, branches and forms are easy to find, and the first direction template is ready to validate before scaling the full content set.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, practical, slightly bold. The brand should feel alive and dopamine-rich without becoming childish, chaotic, or like a generic online education platform.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site look like a regular school, kindergarten, university, or standard LMS. Avoid academic caps, stacks of books, chalkboards as the main image, cartoon robots, generic education icons, emoji, dense card grids, heavy shadows, warm beige/cream surfaces, and unapproved red, pink, purple, or lime accents.

## Design Principles

- Practice is the proof: show learning as action, movement, attempts, routes, corrections, and visible progress.
- One brand, many directions: every direction uses shared navigation, typography, palette, forms, and page logic, while individuality comes from content and topic-specific marks.
- Choose before reading everything: the interface should help visitors quickly pick audience, direction, age, and branch instead of forcing a long brochure scan.
- Energetic but usable: large type, asymmetry, scribbles, and motion must support orientation rather than overload the page.
- No invented proof: prices, addresses, teachers, reviews, phone numbers, and messenger contacts stay neutral until confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard access, semantic markup, and responsive behavior from 320 px. Motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
